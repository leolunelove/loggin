28 NOVEMBER 2026 · LATEST

From stump to woodpile

Walk the length of a fallen tree. Strike a branch to send it and its leaves tumbling. Pale bands on the trunk show where to cut a length you can lift. Keep hitting the same notch until the two pieces part.

Stand beside a short, bare log and press E to lift it into your bag. You can take the first piece home while the rest of the tree waits. Your camp remembers what you left, right down to a half-finished cut.

28 NOVEMBER 2026

Where the birch falls

The tree you fell comes down with its crown, bark and axe marks intact. Walk along it, pick a spot and cut there. When the last fibres give, the two lengths settle against the ground. Your camp remembers where they landed.

Close to a trunk, the woodcutter eases back or to the side to make room. In a tight gap he chokes up on the handle for a shorter stroke. WASD takes over his footing as soon as you move.

28 NOVEMBER 2026

Cut across the grain

Hold right mouse and aim at the bark. Scroll to turn the chalk line, or tap Z and C to adjust it by two degrees. Left-click to swing on that line.

Strike the same groove to deepen it, or turn the blade and cut across it. Both marks stay on the tree when you return to camp and when you load your save.

27 NOVEMBER 2026

Work clothes and fireside rest

Pull on the field shirt, quilted vest or mill apron. Turn your woodcutter in the Outfitter, choose a shave, stubble or a short beard, and check the dyes in daylight or firelight. The same hat, face and pack go into the woods with you.

Birches show the grain under their paper bark. Working the same notch opens it deeper. Back at camp, your loaded pack and axe rest beside you on the bench. The coals glow into the evening, and the finished woodshed gives you a dry spot to listen to the rain.

27 NOVEMBER 2026

Over the shoulder

Hold right mouse to look over your shoulder and steady the axe in both hands. Place the sight on the bark and click to cut there. The ring turns gold when the surface is within reach. Release to return to your walking view. Q still locks a tree.

27 NOVEMBER 2026

Steel against the grain

The edge bites into the notch you’ve cut. Catch the haft on a post and your wrist turns with it. Each axe has its own forged head, ground bevel and shaped wooden handle.

Press X to slip the axe into the leather loops on your pack. A click draws it straight into a swing. It rides there while you unload timber or step inside.

27 NOVEMBER 2026

A woodcutter’s field book

Every useful cut earns Woodcutting XP. Press K to see your level, progress to the next level, and records for each species. Camp orders open at level 5. Later skills reward working the same notch and finishing figured hardwood.

Look down and swing to hew a fallen length. The blade meets wood, leaves, soil or water along its path. Space jumps, Shift sprints, and a heavy bag takes a little more out of you. Three discoveries sew three different patches onto your pack.

27 NOVEMBER 2026

Room to roam

Follow the fingerpost to the orchard, the riverbank or the high woods. There’s now at least 78 metres between any two homes. Your discoveries, repairs and trails carry over.

Walk through any front door and the roof opens as the camera settles overhead. WASD follows the room on screen. Look for the preserving table at Orchard Rest, the oars at Riverwatch and the stove and saw at Pine Hollow.

Saplings bend as you pass and release loose leaves. Standing trunks follow the lean of the timber. When a tree comes down, leaves scatter and the log sections roll and settle on the slope. The stump stays solid.

The journey home

One tap starts a stroke or stacks a bag at the shack. Each bundle knocks against the rack as it lands. Close the workbench to walk straight back into the woods.

Carry timber and the compass points to the nearest drop that can take it. Mark a discovered home under Woodland to keep your own bearing. The carved fingerpost outside the shack points towards all three homes.

Turn the outfitter to Close-up to inspect your cap, face and workwear. Out on the trail, cart wheels turn independently and the axle creaks under load. Sit by the fire and the camera looks for a clear view through the trees.

27 NOVEMBER 2026

Life along the trails

Your connected homes now share a working handcart. Load it yourself or hire a hauler to collect timber. Every log is yours to spend the moment you stack it.

Orchard Rest offers apples and lanterns. Riverwatch has a footbridge and a rack across the water. Pine Hollow improves your axe and axle. Follow old markings for building plans, cut windthrows into sections, and leave a white blaze on trees you want to keep.

The outfitter now shows your lumberjack with their equipped axe, loaded bag and painted clothing. Try a field brim, a watch cap or a rail cap with a work shirt, quilted vest or mill apron. You can change again in Settings.

Homes with room to live

The three woodland homes now have their own architecture: a broad orchard cottage with a veranda, a long riverside cabin with a sloped roof and deck, and a twin-gabled logging lodge with a stone chimney and four beds.

With three to five times the original floor area, these buildings have proper furniture layouts, human-sized doorways, matching collisions and larger interior camera views. Your restoration and connection progress carries over.

Little homes in a bigger wood

Weather in the woods

Rain drifts between the trees beneath cooler, overcast skies, with little splashes underfoot. Take shelter in the shack to hear a close wooden-roof patter over the softened outdoor rain. Completed porch and sawmill roofs offer shelter too.

Some mornings wake under a soft veil of mist that lifts as the day warms. The weather follows your saved camp time, while the fire keeps glowing and your bench remains a place to sit through a shower.

Watch flames dance, embers rise and smoke drift into the trees. The fire throws flickering amber light across camp, with stereo crackling that fades into the distance and softens inside the shack. Your hearth stays lit and saves with your camp.

26 NOVEMBER 2026 · TREE WORK

Better tree work

  • Press Q to lock onto the tree you’re aiming at. Press Q again to release it.
  • Strafe around a locked tree while the camera keeps it in view. Felling it, moving too far away or opening a menu releases the lock.
  • Move after a swing starts. The stroke follows through, with a miss if the trunk is out of reach at contact.
