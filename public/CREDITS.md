## Camp Pennant

The woodland title scene and field notes are captured from Loggin’. The cabin crest and three discovery patches were created for this game with OpenAI image generation. Each discovery patch appears in the woodland journal and on the playable character’s bag. The LOGGIN’ lettering is rendered artwork set in Tanker by Indian Type Foundry. The interface uses Barlow Condensed and DM Sans, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their notices ship in LICENSES/barlowcondensed-OFL.txt and LICENSES/dmsans-OFL.txt.

The cabin illustration prompt accompanies the Camp Pennant study in style-studies/index.html. The prompts for the three discovery patches follow.
