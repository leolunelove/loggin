# Woodsmoke sound recordings

All game effects are served locally. No accounts or external audio service are used during play.

- Axe takes 1–4: edited from **Felling a tree with an axe** by **tomattka**, recorded with Zoom H1, September 8, 2017. Original: https://freesound.org/people/tomattka/sounds/401730/ — CC0 1.0.
- Trunk crack, falling foliage and ground landing: edited from **timber tree falling 2** by **matt_beer**, recorded with Zoom H6, April 27, 2020. Original: https://freesound.org/people/matt_beer/sounds/515312/ — CC0 1.0.

Licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Edits: isolated events, leading silence removed, DC/rumble filtering, gentle low-frequency emphasis, matched hit loudness, gentle transient limiting and peak normalization, short click-free fades, mono 22.05 kHz PCM. Original recordings are credited voluntarily. These are field recordings, replacing Woodsmoke's former synthesized impact tones.
