Woodland Almanac. Original spot illustration for the menus of Loggin’, a cosy autumn forestry game. On a fully transparent background, illustrate just one naturalist's specimen: a gracefully arching twig with three ochre and russet lobed oak leaves and two small acorns. Watercolour with golden translucent washes, fine warm-brown pencil veins, tactile drybrush and a tiny ink contour; like an observation painted outdoors in a well-loved field notebook. A sophisticated, hand-painted diagonal silhouette rising from lower left to upper right, wide composition, about 3:2. Legible when small. The art only, no notebook, no text, lettering, label, border, mockup or cast shadow. Completely original.

Camp Pennant. An original sewn summer-camp patch for a cosy game, one isolated arched shield-shaped embroidered fabric crest, with actual transparent space outside the badge. Inside: a stylized rust-red cabin with a single golden lit window, two dark pine trees and a bent path beside a golden rising sun. Chunky flat interlocking pieces of dark evergreen, salmon terracotta, deep teal, mustard and cream, rich visible dense striped embroidery in each shape and a thick oatmeal merrowed edge. Strong graphic silhouette, jaunty geometric composition and tangible matte thread texture, close up almost square aspect. The crest art only. No lettering, no words, no printed numbers, no surrounding objects, no background, no shadows outside the patch.

Hearth Press. Original small two-colour linocut print illustration for a cosy timber camp game. A stout, inviting timber shack beside a pile of three logs, a striped roof, chimney with a single curling ribbon of smoke, a rough-cut orange sun peeking behind, and two spare spruce silhouettes. Soft irregular gouge marks, lively black hatching and negative cuts that show through to paper; real hand-inked print impression, elegant composition and bold simplification, large unbroken shapes for a legible vignette at small sizes. Only near-charcoal warm ink and faded vermilion ink, transparent background and negative space. Strict black and vermilion, no printed cream backdrop. Original graphic, landscape 3:2, generous transparent edges, isolated unframed art, no letters or text.
